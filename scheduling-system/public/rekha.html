About rekha & Quick Guide

rekha is a modern vector diagramming studio with multi-page tabs and dynamic matrix tables.

1.
Shapes & Connectors: Click or drag any shape from the sidebar onto the canvas. Drag connector handles to link shapes.
2.
Matrix Tables: Right-click on table cells to add/remove rows or columns, merge cells, or adjust text alignments.
3.
Import & Export: Save projects with Ctrl+S as Vector SVG, .rekha, PDF, or PNG, and open them anytime.
4.
Canvas Navigation: Hold Space + drag mouse to pan. Use mouse wheel to zoom in/out.
